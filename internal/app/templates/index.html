A temporary room on your local network. Every device with a browser can join by QR code and share files and notes. When the room's time is up, everything on it is deleted.

Your room

The room, its files and its notes are deleted when this time runs out.
Devices that join must type it before they see anything.
  • Stays on your network. Files go from device to device through this server, never through a cloud.
  • No apps or accounts. A browser and the room's QR code are all another device needs.
  • Gone when time is up. Rooms close on their own; you can also close one early.

About HAMAL

HAMAL moves files and notes between devices on your local network, with no cloud storage, accounts or telemetry.
